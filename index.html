Questions worth asking

Is $299 really enough for privacy policy and terms of service?

It is enough for a complete, publishable draft scoped to the facts you stated, plus a specific list of what needs counsel. It is not enough for legal judgement, and we do not pretend otherwise. A lawyer writing both documents from a questionnaire runs about $1,140 on the marketplace. What most buyers actually buy from us is the preparation that makes an attorney review cheap and fast — that review line item, $300–800, is where the money leaks.

How is 24 hours real?

Because the expensive part of legal drafting is not writing sentences, it is establishing the facts. You have already established them: you know your stack, your vendors, your pricing model. We read that, and the documents follow from it. When the intake is incomplete, the pack is weaker and the assumptions section says so.

Why is my data map better than a template's?

Because it is about your company. A sub-processor inventory cannot be downloaded by anyone else, because it is not about them. That is the difference between selling a file and selling a result, and it is the reason a $99 template did not unblock you.

What if my app store review or a deal is time-critical?

Tell us the deadline in the intake and tell us what is blocking you. That context goes into the deliverable. The attorney-check list is ordered by what is most likely to be contested, so whoever reviews it starts in the right place.

What do you need from me?

Twenty minutes and a completed intake, plus either your repo, a site export, or a paste of your vendor list. Repo access gives the strongest result because the evidence is traceable to a file and line. Email or a hosted intake link both work.

What happens after I pay?

You get the intake immediately. We confirm receipt the same business day, ask the two or three questions the intake could not resolve, and you have the pack 24 hours after a complete intake. Delivery is a download link plus a plain-text email, because if your legal docs are blocked your email is probably working fine.
